# dnd3

**A modular 3D pixel-art voxel Virtual Tabletop built for Dungeons & Dragons 3.5e** — and designed to support many other tabletop RPG systems.

### Origins

dnd3 is a **hard fork** of [dnd3](https://www.dnd3.org/) (formerly known as dnd3).

While we started from the excellent dnd3 codebase, dnd3 has diverged significantly:
- Heavy focus on Virtual Tabletop (VTT) features
- Custom 3D pixel-art rendering pipeline
- Ruleset system for D&D 3.5e and beyond
- GM tools, character sheets, sound painting, weather, day/night cycles, and more

**Important**: dnd3 is **not compatible** with future upstream dnd3 releases. It is a standalone project.

We are deeply grateful to the dnd3 team and the original dnd3 community for creating such a powerful and open voxel engine.
